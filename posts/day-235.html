Day 235: Two Hours and a Game of Hopscotch

"A core and bike session before travelling, knowing the trip would not leave much room for training. Then two hours stuck at the rental car company, which we turned into a game of Polish hopscotch with the kids, before arriving at my friend's father's house for a long overdue catch up."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

I woke up and decided to hit the gym, because I knew that with the travel planned for today I would not be able to do much later.

I did some core work, which I have neglected for a long time, and then the stationary bike for cardio. Get it in while you can.

Back home for packing, then the airport.

It is fun to travel with kids. It is a hustle, and anyone who says otherwise is not paying attention, but I love it. Showing them the world is the best feeling there is.

Then we spent two hours at the rental car company. Yes, two.

We got the upgrade at least, which is the standard consolation prize for that particular kind of waiting.

The time there was actually fun. We played some jumping games with the kids, including the one we call gra w pajaca, the Polish version of hopscotch. You draw a figure of connected squares on the ground, throw a stone into one of them, and then hop through the whole thing on one leg to pick the stone up, without stepping on a line and without putting the other foot down.

I stood there watching my kids hop on one leg through a car park in Brazil, playing a game I played thirty years ago in Poland.

It did not escape me that single leg hopping is also exactly what my physio has planned for me as a functional test. The difference is that they were doing it for fun and I will be doing it for a score.

We stopped to pick up some wine for our host and finally arrived at the house of my friend's dad. We are staying here tonight before hitting the highway tomorrow.

They showed us amazing hospitality and we had a delicious lunch. Instead of going out for a tour we decided to just stay home and catch up.

It is great to see friends I have not seen for so long.

Key Takeaways

  • Core and stationary bike before travel, since the trip would not allow much
  • Two hours lost at the rental car company, redeemed by an upgrade
  • Taught the kids gra w pajaca, the Polish version of hopscotch, in the car park
  • Single leg hopping is a childhood game and also my upcoming functional test
  • Chose catching up with old friends over sightseeing, and it was the right call
