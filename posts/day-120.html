Day 120: Slower Body, Easier Steps

"The day began under the weight of fatigue, with the body clearly asking for less after several full days in a row. Energy stayed below normal, but the knee remained stable and quietly showed another sign of progress: walking is becoming easier and more natural, even on a day when everything else felt slower."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 2/10

The morning started with a hangover, and surprisingly I felt worse than yesterday even though I had drunk less. It made me wonder what exactly was behind it. Maybe sleep, maybe the gym from yesterday, maybe simply the accumulated tiredness of several intense days catching up at once. Whatever the reason, the body clearly asked for a slower rhythm, so I decided not to fight it.

I stayed in and worked through the morning with breakfast and coffee. At first my mind felt slow, almost resistant, but once I sat in front of the computer things gradually accelerated and focus came back. After the team call I went to meet Renato and his friends for lunch. I tried to eat as healthy as possible, but in the US that often feels harder than expected. Everything is generous with sauces, fats, and hidden heaviness. The food is delicious, but often leaves the impression that something in it is too much.

After lunch there was shopping and a few errands, nothing physically demanding, which suited the day well. I did no exercise at all today. The knee felt okay, not especially good, but stable enough not to demand attention. What I did notice again was how much easier walking has become. I still need to consciously remember to activate the muscles sometimes, especially the quads, but it happens more naturally now. The body is beginning to cooperate without needing constant reminders.

Later I went to visit my sister-in-law and her family, then met an old friend from London. Those kinds of meetings always carry something valuable, because catching up with people from another phase of life gives perspective on how differently everyone’s story keeps moving. Some paths accelerate, some slow down, but everyone seems to carry invisible chapters.

The day ended with a dinner that was probably unnecessary near where we are staying. By then I already felt tired and sleepy, and the body clearly did not need more food or more time outside. At least I did not drink any more, which already felt like the right decision.

A funny little detail at the end: Zverev was in the restaurant. Suddenly Miami Open starts to feel very present, as if the city is shifting into tournament mode around us.

Back home and straight to bed.

Finally an early night, and hopefully the sleep will reset everything.

Key Takeaways

  • Fatigue is not always proportional to what happened the day before
  • Walking continues to improve even on slower days
  • Conscious muscle activation is becoming more natural
  • Travel and social rhythm accumulate quietly in the body
  • Sometimes the best decision is simply ending the day early
