Day 206: Back in the Hospital Again

"A day that started with a learning celebration at Kai's school and ended with founders and investors in a room full of ideas. In between there was an MRI for my knee, which put me back in a hospital setting I had almost managed to forget. Not a place I miss, even when the news is good."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

The morning belonged to Kai. His school held a learning celebration, one of those events where the kids show what they have been working on and you get to see them proud of their own effort. It is a good way to start a Wednesday.

Then it was my turn to be examined.

I had an MRI scheduled for my knee, and walking into the hospital again was stranger than I expected. The corridors, the waiting, the paperwork, the particular quiet of those places. I had almost forgotten what all of it feels like. Six months ago this was my routine.

I am not missing it at all.

The scan itself is just lying still and listening to the machine work. Nothing dramatic. But it is a reminder that this recovery is still an open file, still something being measured and checked rather than simply lived.

Later I met my business associates to talk about new ventures and ideas, and to catch up in general. These conversations are becoming a bigger part of my week. There is something energising about sitting with people who are also building things.

Physically, though, I am not well. I have been fighting something for a couple of days now and it has not let go. I tried ginger shots in the hope of pushing it out of my system. Let's hope it helps.

At night there was another event, this time meeting founders and investors, networking, and mostly listening. What I enjoy most in these rooms is hearing about the problems other founders are dealing with. Everyone is stuck on something, and hearing what that something is tells you far more than any polished pitch.

The day ended with me completely drained.

Tired, still a bit sick, but genuinely fulfilled.

Key Takeaways

  • MRI done, and being back in a hospital setting was a sharp reminder of where this started
  • Still fighting an illness, trying ginger shots to speed it up
  • Two separate business meetings in one day, both worth the energy
  • Listening to other founders' problems is more useful than any pitch
  • Exhausted by the end, but the good kind of exhausted
