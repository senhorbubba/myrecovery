Week 25 May 4, 2026

Day 169: Resetting the Routine

"A hectic day focused on reorganizing both the house and personal habits. Between work, family chaos, cardio, and thoughts about health and weight, the day carried a strong feeling of reset. Physically the knee remained stable, but mentally and physically fatigue continued to show through stress and back pain."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

The morning started with the usual chaos, even worse after three days without school. Eventually we managed to get the little monsters out of the house, but it took effort.

No gym in the morning today. Instead, the whole day at home became intense in a different way. We are reorganizing the house and going through clothes and clutter. At one point there were five women folding clothes and reorganizing everything around us. With every hour the house felt lighter and more organized, and honestly so did my mind. At the same time, it was almost impossible to fully focus on work with so much movement and noise around.

Juliana is starting one of her diets again, and seeing the scale lately pushed me to restart my own version as well. I keep thinking about those extra kilos. Today I started organizing a proper menu for the week, focused on protein, lean whole foods, more liquids, and less random eating. I also brought palatinose back into my diet to help stabilize energy during the day.

In the afternoon, Cleyber canceled our session because he had to travel earlier with his athletics team for a competition. Honestly, it was probably for the best. I trained legs hard yesterday and the body still feels it.

Still, I didn't stay inactive. I used the extra time to focus on cardio instead. Forty minutes total: ten minutes biking, ten on the elliptical, ten rowing, and ten running. It felt good to move continuously again and rebuild some conditioning.

Later I took Kai to his tennis training. It's amazing how much this boy enjoys tennis and how quickly he is improving. There is another kid in the group with a very similar level and attitude, and watching them compete while still genuinely enjoying the game is great. Pure competition without pressure yet.

Back home for dinner, but by the evening I started feeling off. Tired, stressed, mentally overloaded, and with my back still bothering me.

One of those days where the knee feels fine, but the rest of the system clearly needs attention too.

Key Takeaways

  • Organizing physical space can also lighten mental space
  • Recovery now includes rebuilding healthier habits overall
  • Cardio is becoming important again beyond rehab alone
  • Watching kids enjoy sport brings motivation and perspective
  • Stress and back pain now compete more with the knee for attention
