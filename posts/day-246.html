Day 246: Too Old for This

"Four hours of sleep after the final, vitals completely off, and a day spent surviving rather than living. Cleyber made me do the physio anyway, and I am glad he did. We ran an isometric strength test comparing the legs, and the result was more encouraging than what I feel in the gym."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

Huge hangover. Four hours of sleep. Vitals completely off.

I am too old for this.

The day went past me rather than with me. I spent most of it simply trying to survive until a reasonable hour, doing the minimum that had to be done.

Cleyber forced me to do the physio.

I am glad he did. I almost cancelled, and I had a well constructed argument ready about how rest would be more valuable than a compromised session. He was not interested in it.

We cannot stop now.

We did an isometric strength test to compare the two legs, which is exactly the kind of objective check I need more of. The result was better than I expected. There is not much difference between the legs in isometric strength.

That is genuinely good news, and it is also only part of the picture.

Because later in the gym I could still feel a big difference between the two sides. Isometric strength is one thing, holding a position against resistance. Producing force through a full range of movement, repeatedly, under fatigue, is something else, and that is where the operated leg still shows its history.

So both things are true. The leg is getting stronger and the numbers are catching up.

And there is still a lot to go.

Pushing.

Key Takeaways

  • Four hours of sleep after the final, and a day spent surviving
  • Cleyber refused to let me cancel the physio session, which was the right call
  • Isometric strength test showed little difference between the legs
  • The gym still reveals a clear difference, because isometric is not the whole story
  • Good numbers and a long way to go are both true at once
