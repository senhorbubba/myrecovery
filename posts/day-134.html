Day 134: Back to Reality

"A return to routine after a week of disruption, with both body and mind feeling the effects. The knee remains functional, but stiffness and familiar discomfort have returned, likely linked to inconsistency. The session with Cleyber brought reassurance and a needed reset in direction."

Pain Level 2/10
Swelling 2/10

Monday morning came with a bit of chaos. After a week of visits, cousins, and being spoiled, the kids were not in the mood to cooperate. I could feel my own patience was also lower than usual, which didn’t help. It took effort, but eventually we managed to get everyone ready and out to school.

Quick breakfast and then straight to the office. I switched into working mode early and stayed focused through the morning. Physically, walking feels easy, but there is that familiar sensation again from last month. The leg feels stiff, with some discomfort on the back right side. It doesn’t feel alarming, more like something that needs attention, probably stretching and consistency.

Back home later, I met the kids after school, then went down to see Cleyber. It had been a couple of weeks since our last session because of travel, so it was good to reconnect and get his view. He was happy with the progress, which gave me some reassurance. The patellar pain is still there, but clearly reduced, and I can feel that in exercises. Squats are easier now, and I can push more weight without hesitation.

He asked about my routine, and I didn’t feel great about my answer. The past week lacked structure, and I know it. Still, I told him I would get back to it, and I meant it.

We moved upstairs to focus on releasing the muscles and working on extension and flexion. Both were in good shape, actually better than I expected, which was a positive surprise. It confirmed that even with some inconsistency, the base progress is still there.

Back home for dinner and to relax. No ice today, as my ice bag is now with my father-in-law, who needs it more for his back. Somehow that felt like the right trade.

A day that reminded me where I stand. Progress is real, but it depends on what I do next.

Key Takeaways

  • Lack of routine quickly shows up in stiffness and discomfort
  • Progress can hold even through short periods of inconsistency
  • External feedback helps recalibrate perspective
  • Mobility remains a strong point in recovery
  • Getting back to structure is now the priority
