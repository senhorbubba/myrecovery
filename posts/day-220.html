Day 220: Nine Holes and One Long Day

"Nine holes walked on an amazing course, and I played better than I expected to. Then driving range hits with the kids, a windy beach, the pool, and a Brazil World Cup game with far too much to drink. A great day that went on about three hours too long."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

Up early, and I decided to try golf properly.

Nine holes, walking, and then some hits with the kids on the driving range afterwards. The course was amazing and it was great to simply walk it and play, which is not something I could have done a few months ago without thinking hard about the consequences.

I played better than I thought I would.

That is becoming a pattern lately. My expectations were set during the worst part of the recovery and I keep forgetting to update them.

Walking a full nine is real work for the legs, and the knee handled all of it. No swelling, no complaints, no moment where I had to think about how I was placing my foot.

Back to the hotel for lunch. The beach was too windy, so we went back up to the pool instead. Then getting ready for the Brazil game in the World Cup.

A lot of beer, wine, and drinks. By the end I was feeling heavy in a way that had nothing to do with training.

Later there was dinner, and finally back to the room.

The day was definitely too long. I started to feel the toll of it, and I could tell tomorrow was going to send me the bill.

Key Takeaways

  • Nine holes walked, with the knee handling the full round comfortably
  • Played better than expected, because my expectations are still stuck in the injury
  • Driving range with the kids afterwards, a good way to share the sport
  • Too much to drink around the Brazil game, and the day ran too long
  • Feeling the accumulated toll of a heavy holiday week
