Day 240: No Gym, Plenty of Motion

"A lazy day on the training front and a busy one on every other. Breakfast with one friend, a client call, lunch with another friend, a coffee meeting, and then France against Spain at the bar. What a game. Home tipsy but happy, and I still managed some stretching."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

The day was super lazy in one sense and completely full in another.

I did not even feel like going to the gym. I was resting after the travel and putting everything into work instead, and I decided not to argue with that.

It turned into a busy day of meetings.

First, breakfast with a friend, getting advice on the business ventures. Then a call at home with a client. Then another meeting with another friend, a quick lunch, looking at new business and possible partners. Then another call at a cafe.

Four conversations before the evening, which is more than I usually manage in a week.

Then France against Spain at the bar. Beers, a great atmosphere, and Spain winning.

What a game.

Back home a little tipsy but happy. I played with the kids, and even did some stretching at the end of it, which given the state of the day I am counting as a genuine win.

No training, a lot of walking, and a lot of talking. Some days move you forward without a single repetition.

Key Takeaways

  • No gym after the travel, and no guilt attached to that
  • Four business conversations in one day, from breakfast through to a late coffee
  • France against Spain was the game of the tournament so far
  • Stretching still happened, despite the state of the evening
  • Some days move things forward with no training at all
