Day 239: Last Tour of Brasilia

"A cancelled client call freed up the afternoon, so I took the kids for one last tour of Brasilia. Impressive buildings, and a strange feeling seeing how many people and how much money it takes to govern the country. Then home to Sao Paulo, and the trip was over."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

I woke up, started working, and packed the suitcases.

I was waiting on a call with a client that eventually got cancelled, which freed up the middle of the day. So I took the kids and we did one last tour around Brasilia.

It is a strange city to walk through.

All these impressive buildings, deliberately monumental, and so many people working inside them governing the country. My honest reaction is that there are definitely too many people here and too much money spent on all of it. That thought is hard to avoid when you see the scale of it laid out in one place.

But it is genuinely worth seeing, and the kids got something out of it.

We had a relaxing afternoon with some drinks at the end, a bit of walking and sightseeing, and then the flight back to Sao Paulo.

We got home tired, and everyone went to sleep quickly.

The trip was over.

Nine days that included two cities, a national park, several hikes, a lot of driving, and almost no proper training. Six months ago a trip like this would have been an exercise in damage limitation. This time the knee was simply not a factor in any decision we made.

Key Takeaways

  • Cancelled client call turned into a last tour of Brasilia with the kids
  • Impressive architecture, and an uncomfortable sense of scale and cost
  • Trip finished with a flight back to Sao Paulo and an early night
  • Nine days of travel with almost no structured training
  • The knee did not influence a single decision on the whole trip
