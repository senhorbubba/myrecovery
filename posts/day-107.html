Day 107: Less Fluid, More Perspective

"The day began with stiffness and local discomfort after yesterday’s procedure, but visible reduction in swelling brought reassurance. Physically, the knee required caution; mentally, the day opened space for reflection, especially around parenting, patience, and how recovery has quietly shaped family dynamics. A lighter rehab session closed the day with a sense of calm and control."

Pain Level 2/10
Swelling 2/10

Morning started with a stiff knee and some pain exactly at the injection point.

Expected, after yesterday.

The outside of the knee looks noticeably smaller now, less fluid there, but the inside still holds swelling. It’s visible and you feel it when moving.

Did a quick gym session early: back and biceps only. No leg work today. Then office, driving, sitting, meetings. I tried to move more during the day. I keep wondering whether movement helps the hyaluronic acid distribute faster or absorb better, or if time is really the only factor.

After work, another meeting, this time with my son’s therapist.

The conversation was supposed to be about him, but in many ways it felt more about the parents. That made me think again how therapy often works less directly on the child and more on the family around him, helping adults understand emotional needs, reactions, and how to respond better.

And in that sense, recovery has unexpectedly helped me too.

I became calmer.

More measured with my wife, with the kids, with daily frustrations.

Apart from those first difficult weeks, this recovery has actually been positive for the family in ways I didn’t expect.

There is something about pain, limitation, repetition, and consistency that changes how you relate to people.

The patience required by rehab seems to spill into parenting.

And maybe the kids also see something important in all this: the repetition, the routines, the boring parts, the discomfort, the fact that you continue anyway.

Not dramatic.

Just steady.

I hope they remember that.

I hope they learn something from watching it.

In the evening I met Cleyber. We discussed everything from yesterday’s appointment with Dr. Sergio. Since the knee was still fresh after the injection, we kept it lighter.

Some exercises, no pain at all - then release work and drainage.

Back home tired, but happy and fulfilled.

Finished with PowerDot massage, leg elevated to help drainage, then ice.

That felt very right.

I think this may become part of the daily routine now.

Key Takeaways

  • Swelling reduction is visible, even if incomplete.
  • Fresh interventions require patience, not overreaction.
  • Recovery has improved emotional control beyond the knee.
  • Parenting often improves when adults slow down first.
  • Kids learn resilience by watching repeated effort.
  • Drainage + elevation + stimulation may become a valuable evening ritual.
