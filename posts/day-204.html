Day 204: Watching Him Grow

"A day with no physio but plenty of movement and emotion. My legs reminded me of yesterday's run, especially the calves, making recovery the priority. The highlight, however, was celebrating my oldest son with his friends and realizing that he is building something much more valuable than grades or trophies—a great group of people around him."

Pain Level 1-2/10
Swelling 1/10

Today there was no physio session. Cleyber is traveling, and to be honest, I probably needed the break anyway. After yesterday's 5 km run, I could really feel my legs today, especially my calves. It's a different kind of soreness than after the gym, reminding me that outdoor running uses muscles that machines cannot fully replicate.

The day was busy from the start. I had to go to school earlier to pick up my oldest son and a group of his friends for a small birthday celebration. His actual birthday is still two weeks away, but school will already be over by then, so we decided to celebrate now while everyone could be together.

Our first stop was an escape room. Watching the kids work together, argue, laugh, and solve problems was almost as entertaining as the activity itself. After that, we went to Benihana, where the flying food, fire tricks, and showmanship kept everyone entertained.

And, of course, there was more wine for the adults.

I keep telling myself that I really have to stop drinking so much. The pattern has become obvious, and while the social moments are worth it, the recovery the next day often is not.

The best part of the day, though, was simply watching my son enjoy himself. He has built an amazing group of friends—kind, funny, supportive kids who genuinely enjoy being together. As a parent, that probably matters even more than academic results or sporting achievements.

By the time we got home, my legs were still sore, but my mind felt light.

Recovery isn't always about another training session.

Sometimes it's about stepping back and appreciating how the people you love are growing alongside you.

Key Takeaways

  • Recovery days are valuable after introducing new physical challenges
  • Outdoor running created soreness that highlights areas still adapting
  • Strong friendships are one of the greatest gifts a child can build
  • Celebrating family milestones is time well spent
  • Balance remains the biggest challenge, especially when it comes to food and alcohol
