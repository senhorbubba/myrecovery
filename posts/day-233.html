Day 233: Stretching Through the Games

"Strength training in the morning, then a short work day from home with one client meeting. The evening was Argentina against Egypt and Switzerland against Colombia, with stretching happening throughout. The routine might genuinely be coming back."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

Strength training in the morning, then work from home.

A quick meeting with a client, and then I wrapped up what turned out to be a short day. Not every week needs to be maximal.

The evening was World Cup again. Argentina against Egypt, and Switzerland against Colombia.

I love the World Cup. There is something about a month where football is simply on, every day, at predictable times, that restructures the whole rhythm of life. You plan around it without deciding to.

And I stretched during the games, of course.

That is not nothing. Two matches is roughly three hours of sitting, and turning that into three hours of floor time is the easiest habit trade I have available. I am not being disciplined, I am being lazy in a useful direction.

Is my stretching routine actually coming back?

It looks like it might be. Three evenings in a row now. I will not call it a routine yet, but I will note that I did not have to argue with myself about it today.

Key Takeaways

  • Strength training in the morning and a short work day after it
  • Stretching during World Cup matches turns sitting time into floor time
  • Three evenings in a row of stretching, which is close to a habit
  • The easiest habits are the ones attached to something already happening
  • Argentina against Egypt and Switzerland against Colombia to close the day
