Day 228: Rebuilding the Small Habits

"An hour in the gym on chest and triceps, then working from home with a few calls. The day ended with stretching, as I try to bring back a routine that used to be automatic during the early months of this recovery."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

Gym in the morning, one hour, chest and triceps.

Then working from home, a few calls, nothing remarkable. The kind of day that does not photograph well but keeps everything moving.

At the end of the day I did some stretches.

I am trying to come back to my stretching routine again, and I keep noticing how much harder it is to rebuild a habit than it was to build it the first time. In the early months after surgery, stretching was not optional. It was the whole day. There was no question about whether it would happen because there was very little else I could do.

Now that I can train properly, the small things are the first to disappear.

That is the trap. The better I get, the more likely I am to skip exactly the work that got me here. Stretching, rolling, the boring maintenance that has no visible payoff on the day you do it.

So I am putting it back, deliberately, one evening at a time.

Key Takeaways

  • One hour of chest and triceps in the morning
  • Stretching routine going back into the schedule on purpose
  • Rebuilding a habit is harder than building it the first time
  • The better the recovery goes, the easier it is to drop the basics
  • Maintenance work has no visible payoff on the day, which is why it gets skipped
