Day 143: Strong Data, Mixed Feelings

"A day where objective signals showed strong recovery, good sleep score, improved strength, and better movement, while subjective feeling lagged behind. The body is progressing, measurements confirm it, but energy and mood still fluctuate. Progress is becoming measurable, even if it is not always fully felt."

Pain Level 2/10
Swelling 1/10

I woke up with a 95% sleep score, probably a record. According to my watch, the sleep quality was excellent. The strange part is that I didn’t really feel like it. I was still a bit annoyed, slightly tired, not fully aligned with what the data was showing.

I had a quick breakfast while watching the Monte Carlo Open. Medvedev losing 6-0 6-0 to Berrettini was shocking. I remember the last time I lost like that, I was probably twelve. It’s a terrible feeling, no matter the level.

I went to the office and after one more coffee, I finally switched into efficient mode. The kind of focus that was missing yesterday came back, and the day started to flow better.

I got back home around 17:00 and went straight to meet Cleyber. We pushed again today. This time we added bands around my waist and worked on more tennis-specific movements. These exercises are getting closer to real sport, and I can feel it. I feel stronger, more stable, more capable.

There is still some pain in the patella, but much less than before, and it no longer limits how much I can push. That is a big shift.

He was happy with my bending. I still feel it could be better, but from his perspective it is almost there. We also measured my leg. Around the knee, the difference is about 4.88%, and it increases up to 8.5% toward the thigh. Not perfect, but within a range that can be worked on.

Seeing those numbers makes things more real. Progress is not only a feeling now, it is something measurable.

Back home, I watched a movie and did a bit more work. I had to finish a few things since I won’t be working tomorrow.

Then finally, sleep.

The body is moving forward. The mind is still catching up.

Key Takeaways

  • Objective progress does not always match subjective feeling
  • Strength and control are clearly improving
  • Pain is decreasing without limiting performance
  • Measurements provide clarity and direction
  • Consistency is turning progress into something measurable
