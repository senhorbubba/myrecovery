Day 229: Never Giving Up

"Another solo tennis session full of drills, moving faster and leaning on the legs more than before. The reactions are still delayed and the timing is still off, but it improves every week. In the evening, watching Cabo Verde resist Argentina was a useful reminder about what fight actually looks like."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

Tennis alone again, similar to last week.

A lot of drills with the coach, trying to move faster and putting real demand on the legs. That is the whole point of these sessions now. It is not about the ball, it is about whether the leg gets me there in time.

I still feel a delay in my reaction, and the timing is still off.

But it improves, which is the part that matters. Every week the gap between what I intend to do and what my body actually does gets a little smaller. Slowly, and not in a straight line, but consistently.

Back home, shower, recharged, and out into town for two more meetings.

In the evening I watched Argentina against Cabo Verde. It was remarkable to see how much resistance a small island team could put up against the world champions.

It is all in the will to fight and never give up.

I can relate to that more than I could have a year ago. There is a version of every difficult situation where the gap in ability is real and undeniable, and the only variable left is whether you keep showing up anyway.

That is most of what this recovery has been.

Key Takeaways

  • Solo drills session focused on speed and leg load
  • Reaction time and timing still lag, but improve week over week
  • Two business meetings after the training and a shower
  • Cabo Verde's resistance against Argentina was a lesson in fight
  • When ability is not the variable, showing up is
