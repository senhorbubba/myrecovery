Day 142: Body Up, Energy Down

"A day where the body responded well to movement, but energy levels lagged behind. Training and walking felt easier, signaling continued progress, yet fatigue and low energy took over by the evening. It became clear that recovery now depends not only on training, but also on how I fuel and manage the day."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

I woke up with a bit of a struggle, which felt strange because I had slept long. Something was off from the start, but I decided to go to the gym anyway. Back and biceps today. The session helped immediately. As often happens, once I start moving, the body wakes up and everything feels better.

After that, I sent the kids to school and went for a quick fifteen-minute massage. Back home, I worked from there since I had a lunch scheduled with a friend. It was great to catch up. They just had their second baby, and the house is in full chaos mode, with a lot of people around helping. It puts things into perspective. My friend is dealing with a hernia in the lower back, which made my own situation feel much smaller for a moment.

Lunch was good, maybe too good. I ended up eating sweets again, and I can feel that this is becoming a pattern I need to address. It doesn’t help with how I feel later in the day.

Back home, I finished some work and thought about going for a swim, but there was no time. The kids came back, and we watched the Champions League game, Real Madrid against Bayern. After that, I took my boys for a short walk. Walking feels easier now, more natural, less effort. That is a strong signal. I am coming back.

But when I got home, everything dropped.

I felt strongly annoyed, tired, and weak. My head was spinning a bit, and my energy was gone. The knee was fine, which almost made it more frustrating. It is not the injury anymore, it is something else.

Diet is clearly part of it.

I tried to read a book, but after ten minutes my eyes were closing.

I went to sleep just after 22:00.

Key Takeaways

  • Physical progress does not guarantee stable energy levels
  • Diet is starting to directly affect how I feel
  • Training continues to improve how the body responds
  • Walking is becoming natural again
  • Recovery now depends on lifestyle, not just rehab
