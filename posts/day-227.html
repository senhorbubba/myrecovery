Day 227: The Gap Is Getting Smaller

"A strong session with Cleyber after a long gap, including pivoting work and a lot of movement. He was very happy with the results, and the measurements confirmed it. The difference between the two legs is smaller than it was, which is real progress, though there is still a long way to go."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

Training with Cleyber today, and I had not seen him for a long time.

It was a strong session. Pivoting and a lot of movement work, the specific patterns that tennis actually demands rather than the isolated exercises that dominated the early months. Pivoting in particular was off the table for a long time, so having it back in the programme is significant.

He was very happy with the results.

We also measured the legs, which is the part I always want and always slightly dread. Feelings are unreliable. A tape measure is not.

The difference between the two legs is smaller than it was.

That is the headline, and it matters more than it might sound. For months the operated side was visibly and measurably behind, and no amount of effort seemed to move the number. Now the gap is closing. There is muscle there that was not there before, and it has shown up in the measurement rather than just in how I feel.

But there is still a long way to go.

The two legs are not the same yet, and I can feel that as clearly as I can read it. Some of the asymmetry has stubbornly refused to move, and that is the part the next phase of work has to attack directly.

So it is good news with a caveat, which is how most of this recovery has gone. Real progress, properly measured, and still short of where it needs to be.

I will take it. Six months ago the measurement was a reason to feel bad. Today it is a reason to keep going.

Shopping afterwards, then dinner with the kids, and a properly relaxed evening.

Key Takeaways

  • Strong session with Cleyber including pivoting work, which was off limits for months
  • Leg measurements show the gap between the two sides is genuinely closing
  • There is muscle now that was not there before, confirmed by measurement not just feel
  • The legs are still not symmetrical, and that is the focus of the next phase
  • The measurement has gone from a source of frustration to a source of motivation
