Day 211: Cleared, But Not Ready

"Dr Sergio reviewed the MRI and was genuinely happy with the healing, more advanced than expected, and cleared me to return to sport with caution. He also warned me that the next injury is most likely to come in the other knee. The day ended with hyaluronic acid infiltration in both knees, painful and expensive, but probably worth it."

Pain Level 3/10
Swelling 2/10

Monday, and still a bit hungover from the weekend.

The morning was the maracuja project at school. Maracuja is passion fruit, and the whole idea is a play on the word, giving students a chance to show something they are genuinely passionate about. My son designed a green, sustainable school. He is deep into drawing and architecture right now, and seeing him present his own idea to a room made me really proud. The other kids presenting their passions was just as good to watch.

Back home for some quick work, then lunch with a friend I had not seen in a while. His solar business is going well, which was a nice follow up to my son's sustainable school. The theme of the day picked itself.

Then the appointment I had been waiting for.

Dr Sergio is generally happy with the progress. He said it looks much better, went through the MRI scans with me, and explained how everything is healing. According to him it is more advanced than it should be at this point.

He is freeing me to do my sports, with caution.

Of course, I am not actually ready. I know that. I still have to get my muscles back, and no scan is going to do that part for me. Being cleared and being ready are two very different things.

He also gave me a warning I had not thought about. The second knee is the one most likely to be injured next. We become so protective of the operated leg that we leave the other one carrying too much stress for too long.

That reframed a few things for me.

He asked me to do strength tests and gave me collagen to take. Then we did an infiltration of hyaluronic acid in both knees.

Painful. Expensive. But I suspect worth it.

Key Takeaways

  • Dr Sergio reviewed the MRI and says the healing is more advanced than expected
  • Cleared to return to sport with caution, though clearance is not the same as readiness
  • The other knee is now the higher risk, because we overprotect the operated side
  • Hyaluronic acid infiltration in both knees, plus collagen and strength tests to come
  • My son presenting his sustainable school design was the best part of the day
