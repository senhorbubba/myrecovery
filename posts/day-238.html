Day 238: Wrong Turn, Right Waterfall

"Feeling guilty about doing nothing but hiking, I got up for a forty minute run around the condominium and a light physio session with elastics. Then we drove to the wrong place, lost over an hour, and still ended the day at a beautiful waterfall."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

I woke up feeling guilty that I had not done much training beyond the hiking.

That guilt is not entirely rational, since two days of serious walking is real work, but it got me out of bed. A quick run around the condominium, about forty minutes, and later a light physio session with elastics.

Then we decided to go for one more hike.

We went to the wrong place. Completely the wrong place, and it cost us more than an hour in the car working out where we actually needed to be.

Eventually we got there.

It turned out to be a lovely hike that ended at a beautiful waterfall with a lake we jumped into. Then the walk back up, which is always the honest part of any waterfall hike. The descent is free and the ascent collects the debt.

I was feeling tired by then, and this time it was general fatigue rather than anything to do with the knee.

We grabbed a quick lunch and were back in the car on the way to Brasilia.

In the evening we met my friend's family at a restaurant. A good ending to a day that had spent an hour going in the wrong direction.

Key Takeaways

  • Forty minute run plus light physio with elastics before the day started
  • Guilt about hiking instead of training got me out of bed, even if unfounded
  • Over an hour lost driving to the wrong trailhead
  • The waterfall hike was worth the detour, though the climb back collected its debt
  • Fatigue today was general tiredness, not knee related
