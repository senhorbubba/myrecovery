Day 172: The Night Before the Return

"A day that started with energy, movement, and improved mood, but ended with accumulated fatigue from pushing too hard. Emotionally, though, there was a different kind of energy building underneath everything: excitement. Tomorrow marks the return to tennis."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

I woke up early and went for a quick gym session followed by another massage. Once again, the focus was mainly on my back, and I can finally feel that it is getting much better. Less tension, less stiffness, less constant discomfort.

My mood was better today too.

After going upstairs to help send the kids to school, I realized Juliana had a few calls and meetings, so instead of staying home I went back down to the gym for a second session. This time I focused entirely on legs and more dynamic movements. Side steps, bands, squats, controlled movement patterns. Around forty minutes in total.

And honestly, it felt good.

The body is responding much better now. The movements feel more athletic and less robotic.

Afterward I got back home and started working, but the traffic later in the day completely destroyed my mood again. It took almost an hour, and I could feel my patience disappearing inside the car. Funny how quickly external things can still affect me mentally.

Fortunately, lunch changed the tone of the day. I met a friend visiting Brazil, and good conversations and laughs always help reset my perspective. Sometimes all you need is to sit down with someone who knows you well.

Back to work afterward, and later in the evening we went to a birthday party for friends' kids. A bit of wine, conversations, kids running around everywhere, relaxed atmosphere.

When we got back home, I actually felt lighter and happier.

Then, once the kids finally went to bed, the fatigue hit me hard. I think I simply overtrained this morning and my body finally demanded recovery.

I went to bed early knowing I needed proper sleep.

But underneath the tiredness there was another feeling growing.

Excitement.

Tomorrow is my tennis comeback.

Key Takeaways

  • Mood and physical energy are becoming more connected again
  • Dynamic movements feel increasingly natural and athletic
  • Social interactions continue to improve mental balance
  • Overtraining is easier now that the body feels stronger
  • The return to tennis feels emotionally significant beyond sport
