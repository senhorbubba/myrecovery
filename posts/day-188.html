Day 188: Celebrating Together

"A day that balanced training, family emotions, and time as a couple. Watching Gabriel's team pull off an incredible comeback was a reminder of why we love sports, while celebrating our wedding anniversary brought a chance to slow down and appreciate everything we have been through together. Sometimes recovery also means giving yourself permission to simply enjoy life."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

Saturday started with a gym session focused on back and biceps. Nothing extraordinary, just another consistent step in the process. These sessions have become part of my routine, almost automatic now, and I can feel the cumulative effect they are having on my body.

From there, we headed to Gabriel's football semifinal. What a finish.

His team was losing 0-1 with only three minutes left on the clock. It looked over. Then suddenly they scored the equalizer and, almost immediately after, found another goal to win the game. An unbelievable comeback. The joy on the kids' faces made the whole morning worthwhile.

Back home, it was time for something completely different.

Juliana and I had planned a date to celebrate our wedding anniversary. We started at a small punch bar, a charming place run by a couple from Japan, with an atmosphere that made the evening feel special from the beginning. After that, we went for sushi.

Amazing food.

Amazing drinks.

Probably too much of both, but absolutely worth it.

The past months have been so focused on recovery, schedules, kids, and routines that taking time just for the two of us felt important. Looking back, I realize that this recovery journey has not been mine alone. Juliana has lived every stage of it with me.

By the end of the night, I could also feel that my leg appreciated the slower pace. After several weeks of increasing training volume, today became more about rest than pushing.

And sometimes that is exactly what recovery needs.

Key Takeaways

  • Consistency in training is creating steady progress
  • Sports continue to provide unforgettable family moments
  • Celebrating relationships is an important part of overall wellbeing
  • Recovery is easier when shared with the people who support you
  • Rest is not lost time but an essential part of getting stronger
