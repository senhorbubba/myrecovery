Day 174: Full Circle Moments

"A busy and active day centered around the kids, sport, and family connection. Watching Kai compete and later sharing tennis moments together created a strong sense of continuity between generations. Even without a formal workout, the day was filled with movement, energy, and emotional reward."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

It was an early wake-up today. Kai had to be at school early with his football team, so I went with him. Watching him play is always special. He is very ambitious, competitive, focused. Honestly, he reminds me a lot of myself at his age.

There is something beautiful about seeing your own traits appear naturally in your kids.

After around two hours, we headed back home and crossed paths with Gabriel and Juliana returning from school. We didn't stay long because after lunch we had to head back again for another tennis event at the school.

So much movement today without even planning it.

At the tennis event, I hit a few balls again with Kai. Still very carefully. He keeps pushing me more and more to move, run, and react naturally, and I constantly have to remind myself not to get carried away. The excitement of being back on court sometimes makes me forget that recovery is still happening.

We got back home afterward and I realized I never had time to properly go to the gym today. Still, the day felt physically active from beginning to end. Walking, standing, moving around constantly.

In the evening, our neighbor came over with her kids and we spent time together playing board games. More noise, more movement, more chaos, but the good kind.

A bit of wine later, everyone slowly relaxed and the evening softened.

Today felt good.

Not because of training or milestones, but because I spent real quality time with my boy and later with the whole squad together.

Key Takeaways

  • Sharing sports with the kids creates meaningful connection
  • Returning to tennis requires emotional control as much as physical caution
  • Active days do not always need structured training
  • Family energy can be exhausting and fulfilling at the same time
  • Recovery feels more rewarding when life starts feeling normal again
