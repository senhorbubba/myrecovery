Day 219: Trapeze and a First Bank Account

"A very early run to make space for Juliana's gym session, then a day of circus activities with the kids. All three of them went up on the trapeze and pushed through real fear to do it. It is also Gabriel's birthday, and his gift was his first bank account."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

I ran super early today, because Juliana wanted to go to the gym later and one of us had to be with the kids.

Then I got them ready and we went down to breakfast together.

Later we did activities with them, circus and bow and arrows. Three of them went on the trapeze, which was much higher than it looked from the ground. There was a lot of fear, visible and real, and they all managed to overcome it.

Watching that happen is something else.

It is not about the trapeze. It is about them finding out that being afraid and doing it anyway is a thing they are capable of. That lesson is going to be worth more than most of what I can teach them directly.

After that we did some running with the kids and just enjoyed the good weather.

It is Gabriel's birthday, a special day. We gave him his first bank account as a gift, which sounds unromantic until you see how seriously a kid takes being trusted with something real.

We stayed up late with a lot of drinks in the evening, and there was a festa junina for the kids.

They loved every second of it.

Key Takeaways

  • Early run to fit training around the family schedule
  • All three kids overcame real fear on the trapeze
  • Learning to act despite fear matters more than the activity itself
  • Gabriel's birthday marked with his first bank account
  • Festa junina closed the day, and the kids loved it
