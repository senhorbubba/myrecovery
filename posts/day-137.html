Day 137: Pushing, Feeling, Adjusting

"A structured day built around training, with a return to cardio and a clear test of limits. Strength work felt solid, while running still exposed gaps that need patience. The body handled the load overall, and fatigue at the end of the day felt earned rather than concerning."

Pain Level 2/10
Swelling 1/10

The day started with a morning gym session focused on legs and shoulders. I didn’t manage to complete everything I had planned, but since I was working from home, I already had in mind that I could come back later and finish it properly.

Working from home made the day easier to structure. After lunch, I went back to the gym to complete the session and finally add some cardio, which I have been missing. I spent time on the bike and then tried running again.

Running is still a challenge. I kept it slow, controlled, but after around seven minutes I started to feel pain in my knee, actually in both knees. It is not sharp or alarming, but enough to signal that this is still a weak point. Even so, I finished the session and completed my leg work, pushing a bit more than usual to compensate for the missed session yesterday.

By the time I got home, the kids were already back from school, and the day shifted into family mode. Grandparents came to visit, which turned the evening into the usual mix of noise, conversations, and a bit of chaos. We ended up watching a movie together, which helped slow things down.

Physically, the knee feels good overall. Stable, without any major complaints. The muscles, on the other hand, are clearly tired, which is expected after a day like this.

It feels like progress. Not perfect, not complete, but moving forward.

Key Takeaways

  • Running still requires patience and gradual progression
  • Strength work continues to improve and feels more stable
  • Cardio remains the missing piece that needs consistency
  • Fatigue in muscles is a positive sign of proper workload
  • Progress is visible even when limitations remain
