Day 177: Building Strength and Ideas

"A physically demanding but rewarding day where increasing training load continued to feel manageable. Beyond recovery, the day also opened a new type of project with Gabriel, combining creativity, technology, and early lessons about business and responsibility."

Pain Level 1-2/10
Swelling 1/10

I woke up a bit later than usual, and the only massage session available was at 7:00, so I decided to go hard in the gym before it. I trained legs intensely and then asked the massage therapist to focus completely on releasing the muscles afterward.

Painful.

I could clearly feel the accumulated load from Sunday combined with today's session. The muscles are carrying much more work lately, but in a good way. Tired, sore, alive.

Back home, quick shower, breakfast, and then off to work. Even with the commute, my mood was good today. There is something satisfying about starting the day knowing the physio and training are already done.

We got home earlier than usual, and somehow I still found time to complete the upper-body session I had skipped in the morning, chest and triceps. The day felt busy from start to finish, but one thing is becoming clear: my training load is increasing and my body is handling it well.

That feeling gives me confidence.

The evening became something completely different though.

Gabriel is still extremely excited about the 3D printer, so I decided to use that excitement to teach him some basic business concepts. Together we started building a small "production company" idea where he can take simple orders from friends and create small printed items for them.

It was surprisingly fun to brainstorm products together. The crazy part is that with these printers, it really feels like you can create almost anything.

We spent the evening designing ideas, printing demos, and talking about products, costs, and possibilities.

A different kind of training today, not for the body, but maybe for the future.

Key Takeaways

  • The body is adapting well to higher training loads
  • Muscle soreness now reflects growth rather than limitation
  • Consistency creates confidence in recovery progress
  • Teaching through shared excitement feels natural and effective
  • Small projects can become meaningful lessons about creativity and business
