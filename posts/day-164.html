Day 164: The Weight of Recovery

"A reflective day focused not only on recovery progress, but also on the physical changes that come with months away from full activity. Seeing the number on the scale brought some frustration, but also a realization that the body is rebuilding itself in multiple ways at once."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

The morning started in the usual way, but this time I felt properly rested. Before breakfast, I stepped on the scale and honestly got a bit scared. The number was much higher than I expected, around two kilos above what I normally consider my standard weight.

At the same time, maybe this is simply reality now. No more high school body.

Part of it is definitely positive. I can feel the muscle mass coming back, especially in my upper body and legs, but unfortunately fat seems to be coming back alongside it. I keep trying to understand why this happens so easily during recovery. Maybe it's the lower overall movement, maybe the body reacting differently, maybe slower metabolism because of less muscle activity earlier in recovery, or simply fewer calories being burned fighting constant inflammation.

Probably all of it together.

Since I was working from home today and already thinking about the weight all morning, I decided to squeeze in some cardio after lunch and went to the pool. It had been a while since my last swim, and I immediately felt how out of shape I am. Swimming exposed it clearly. I really need to do it more consistently.

In the evening I met with Cleyber, but we only had thirty minutes, so instead of pushing exercises we focused entirely on muscle release and massage. Extremely painful. I could feel how much tension and soreness was still sitting in the muscles after Monday's harder session.

Naomi came with me because Juliana had dinner with her friends. At one point, Naomi even started helping Cleyber with the massage, which was honestly super cute and made the whole session lighter.

Back home, kids to bed, then rest, some movies, stretching, and early sleep again.

Today reminded me that recovery changes the body in ways beyond the knee itself.

Now it's time to slowly bring balance back again.

Key Takeaways

  • Recovery affects body composition as much as physical performance
  • Strength gains can coexist with frustration about weight
  • Swimming exposed how much cardio conditioning was lost
  • Muscle tension reflects the increased training intensity lately
  • Balance now includes rebuilding fitness, not only healing the knee
