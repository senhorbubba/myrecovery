Day 122: Long Day on the Move

"A day shaped by movement, changes in plans, and long hours on the feet. Despite a rough start with less sleep, the day unfolded through travel, social moments, and unexpected adjustments. The knee held up overall but started to signal fatigue after extended standing and walking."

Pain Level 2/10
Swelling 2/10

I woke up early to pick up my wife and her friend arriving in Miami, but delays changed the plan and she ended up taking a taxi instead. That cost me a couple of hours of sleep, which I could feel immediately, but I was still happy she had arrived safely. There wasn’t much time to stay at home, as Felipe and I had plans to attend an event connected to the Miami Open.

We got picked up by his friend, a professional tennis player who is constantly on tour. It was interesting to exchange a few stories along the way, getting a glimpse into that lifestyle again. The event itself was set in a very nice house with a tennis court, and the idea was to have a tennis clinic. Unfortunately, the weather didn’t cooperate. Rain and cold made playing impossible, so instead of tennis the event turned into networking, drinks, and conversations.

Originally, the plan was to meet Renato at the Miami Open afterward, but with the weather not improving we decided to head back and meet our wives instead. On the way we stopped for a quick lunch and then met them at a shopping mall. We ended up walking quite a lot without really buying anything, then sat down for a drink at a bar.

At some point during all that movement, I started to feel my knee more clearly. It wasn’t pain in a sharp sense, but a buildup from too much standing and walking throughout the day. It was a reminder that even though things are improving, there is still a limit to how much the knee can absorb in one go.

Back home, Felipe and his wife decided to go out again, but we chose a different approach. We stayed in, watched a quick movie, and went to sleep early. It felt like the right decision after a long day.

Before bed, I used PowerDot and ice to settle everything.

A full day, slightly off balance, but managed well.

Key Takeaways

  • Less sleep immediately impacts energy and perception of the day
  • Plans change, and flexibility keeps the day enjoyable
  • Long periods of standing still accumulate stress on the knee
  • Knowing when to stop is as important as staying active
  • Recovery is also about choosing rest when needed
