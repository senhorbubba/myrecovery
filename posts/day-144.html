Day 144: Celebration and Contrast

"A birthday filled with energy, family, and meaningful moments, balancing intention with indulgence. The day started with discipline and care, then naturally shifted into celebration. Physically, the body held up well through a full day of activity, including a return to golf, while mentally the focus stayed on enjoying the people and the moment."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

It’s my birthday today. I decided to start the day in a healthy way, knowing that it would probably end differently.

I went to the gym in the morning, and in the middle of the session I got surprised by my kids who came downstairs to wish me happy birthday. They had prepared a small game with clues to help me find my present. It was super cute, one of those moments that stays with you. We even had cake right there in the morning, which officially marked the start of a less controlled day.

After that, I sent them to school and made a conscious decision not to work. Instead, I went for a sauna session. I really enjoy that time. It allows me to slow down, think, and just be present without distractions. It always leaves me feeling better, both physically and mentally.

After the sauna, my wife had arranged a massage for me. The therapist went deep, probably deeper than expected, but it left me feeling very good afterward, loose and relaxed.

We then went out for a birthday lunch in the center of São Paulo. Great food, great atmosphere, and we even ran into some friends along the way. It felt natural, unplanned, and easy.

Later in the afternoon, it was time for golf. We chose a par 3 course, something lighter and more manageable. It has been almost six months since I last played properly, apart from a few swings in Miami. It was not easy to get back into it. Timing, control, confidence, everything felt a bit off. Still, I enjoyed it. Not because of performance, but because of being there again and sharing it with good company.

Back home, the celebration continued. Sparkling wine, more time with the family, and then a dinner at a Japanese restaurant with the kids. Loud, messy, full of energy, but also full of life.

It was a great birthday.

A mix of healthy and unhealthy, discipline and indulgence, but most importantly full of laughs and surrounded by the right people.

Key Takeaways

  • Balance does not mean perfection, it means awareness
  • Returning to sport is as much emotional as physical
  • Recovery can coexist with celebration when managed
  • Meaningful moments often come from simple surprises
  • The people around you define the quality of the day
