Week 27 May 21, 2026

Day 186: Momentum

"A day fueled by the optimism from yesterday's appointment with Dr. Sergio. The positive medical feedback carried into training, work, and mindset. Recovery no longer feels like a distant project but something tangible and measurable, with progress becoming visible in both strength and confidence."

Pain Level 1/10
Swelling 1/10

I worked from home today, but started the morning in the gym. It was a quick session because Juliana was taking the kids to school and I needed to help with breakfast before they left.

Once the house was quiet, I went back down for a massage. Once again, the focus was on my back. The difference compared to a few weeks ago is remarkable. The pain is much smaller now and I feel noticeably looser throughout the day. It's one of those things that improves gradually enough that you barely notice until you stop and compare.

After some work at home, I headed out for a meeting to discuss new business models for my consultancy. The conversation was productive, but as often happens these days, the topic eventually drifted toward my knee and recovery. What struck me was how different the tone of those conversations has become.

Everything feels optimistic now.

Time flies. I honestly struggle to remember how dark some of those early weeks felt. Back then, every day seemed long and uncertain. Now the weeks disappear and the focus is on what is possible rather than what is missing.

Back home, I met with Cleyber in the evening. I was still carrying the positive energy from yesterday's appointment with Dr. Sergio, and it definitely showed during the session.

We hit the legs hard.

A lot of exercises, a lot of work, and a lot of progress. Strength continues to improve and movements that felt difficult a few months ago are becoming routine. The gap is still there, but it is shrinking.

After training, I came home knowing I was on my own with the kids for the evening. Fortunately, everything went smoothly. Dinner, bedtime routine, stories, and somehow everyone ended up in bed early.

That left me with something rare.

A bit of quiet time.

And after a day like today, it felt well earned.

Key Takeaways

  • Positive medical feedback creates momentum beyond physical recovery
  • Back pain continues to improve through consistent treatment
  • Recovery feels increasingly focused on performance rather than healing
  • The difficult early months are starting to feel distant
  • Progress is easier to see when confidence grows alongside strength
