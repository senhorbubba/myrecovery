Day 127: Back Home, New Concern

"A slow return day after travel, with low energy but a welcome feeling of being back home. Training was light, but a new sharp sensation on the meniscus side appeared and stayed present through parts of the day. The body feels generally stable, but this new signal brought some concern."

Pain Level 3/10
Swelling 2/10

We finally got back home. The day moved slowly, as expected after travel and lack of sleep. I tried to work in the morning, but my head was not fully there. It felt disconnected, as if part of me was still in transit.

Around 14:00 I decided to go see Taka. It had been a while, and I wanted to reset things after the trip. The session started lightly with mobility and massage, then moved into some simple exercises using bands and shadow swings. Nothing too aggressive, just controlled movements to reconnect.

Then I felt it.

A sharp pain on the operated meniscus side.

It was immediate and different from the usual discomfort I have been feeling over the past weeks. I asked Taka about it, and he did not seem worried, which helped calm me a bit in the moment. Still, the sensation stayed with me for the rest of the day, appearing depending on how I moved.

After the session, since we had not pushed much, I went for a quick gym session. Very short, around twenty minutes, including some light cardio. During that time I felt okay, no real pain, just general fatigue from the day.

I walked back home. Walking itself felt easy, which is reassuring, but I kept thinking about that specific sideways movement that triggered the pain earlier. It stayed in my head more than in my body.

Back home I worked a bit more, then the kids arrived. That moment changed everything. I realized how much I had missed them. I gave them presents, spent time playing, and later took Kai to his tennis practice. While watching him, I did a few squats and small exercises, trying to stay active.

At one point I touched the area around the meniscus again and felt the pain. It is not constant, but it is there. Enough to make me pay attention.

Back home, dinner and early bed. I tried to read a book, but after two pages I fell asleep.

Completely exhausted.

Key Takeaways

  • Travel fatigue affects both body and perception
  • New pain signals need attention, even if others are not concerned
  • Walking remains stable, which is a positive sign
  • The mind can amplify concern when something feels different
  • Being back home and with family resets energy quickly
